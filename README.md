# The TRION

## Project Description

- Project by: Anawin Chaichana
- Game Genre: Puzzle, Adventure

The TRION is a 2D point-and-click adventure puzzle game. Players wake up inside a ruined research facility with no memory of who they are. To escape and uncover the truth, they must navigate six zones of the facility. Each zone have three time periods (Past: 1986, Present: 2026, and Future: 2066). Collecting items, solving environmental puzzles, and piecing together a hidden narrative.

## Tutorial / Usage

**Main Menu**
- Click **Start** or press `Enter` / `Space` to begin
- Click **Quit** or press `Esc` to exit

**In-Game Controls**
- **Left click** — interact with objects and collect items
- **Left click on inventory slot** — select / deselect an item to hold it
- **Time Slider (top-right)** — appears when you hold the Trion Tuner: click Past / Present / Future to shift time

**How to play**
1. Wake up and explore the facility
2. Pick up the **Trion Tuner** from the present-time Central Command
3. Use the Time Slider to switch between time periods and investigate each zone
4. Collect items and use them on objects. Many puzzles require bringing something from one era to use in another
5. Follow the dialogue clues to progress through all six puzzles and find a way out

---

## Game Features

- **Time Travel** — shift between Past (1986), Present (2026), and Future (2066) in any of the six zones at any time
- **6 Zones** — Central Command, Experiment Facility, Biological Research, Director's Office, Floor B1, Quantum Reactor
- **6 Puzzle Chains** — each puzzle requires cause-and-effect reasoning across different time periods
- **Inventory System** — carry and select up to 9 items: selected item is used automatically when you click a compatible object
- **Narrative Dialogue** — story is revealed through in-world dialogue as you explore and solve puzzles
